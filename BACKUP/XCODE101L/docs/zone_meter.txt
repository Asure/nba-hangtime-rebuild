Team Zone
=========

As seen at https://www.bunnyears.net/hangtime/hangscreendunk.jpg there are
meters below both teams marked 'team zone'. This never made it to production for unknown
reasons. The assets still exist and code is available commented in the src.

This option can be enabled at compile time with SYS.EQU setting TEAMZONE (0 or 1)

How:
It's about team play, you can't score all own your own. A pass between players is needed.
The meter is quite slow to fill, but its status stays between quarters.



Where:

or give out some reward.
